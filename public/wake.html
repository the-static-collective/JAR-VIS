03 / WHAT THE EXPERIMENT CANNOT KNOW

  
Timing, circumstances, attention, available energy, safety and trust all influence whether an invitation is useful. A reply is not proof of commitment. An operator's attestation is not independent proof. We are measuring opportunities that led to action, not separating people into superior and inferior classes.
