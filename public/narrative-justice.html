Who holds the pen
when the story becomes a world?

The stranger is not a character. The storyteller is not the proprietor of another person's life.

STORYSHIP helps someone navigate an attributable passage without becoming its owner. Story DNA proposes fictional relationship echoes without acquiring crossover rights. WebZ provides a four-door story entrance; lemonPRESS retains separate editorial authority. JAR;VIS attends the gaps between those instruments and the rights of real people.

Six questions that cannot be answered by code

01 / AUTHORSHIP

Who owns this account?

Can someone say what is shared, to whom, in which medium, and for what purpose?

02 / REFUSAL

Can they remain unrecorded?

Participation in food, community, or art should never require agreeing to appear in a film.

03 / DISSENT

Can they disagree?

Competing accounts should remain visible without falsifying the source record.

04 / EDITORIAL RIGHTS

Who may release it?

Recording permission, creative reuse, derivative works, publication, and correction are separate decisions.

05 / PRACTICAL CARE

What was actually helpful?

Software receipts and good stories do not establish real meals, safer housing, or independently verified care.

06 / INDEPENDENT TABLE

Can people write the sequel?

Can participants refuse the founding story or establish their own gathering and rules?

The real source owners remain separate

STORYSHIP / navigation turn · Story DNA / relationship echo · WebZ / narrative doorway · lemonPRESS / publisher

What this is not

Not a documentary release form, a community vote, a grant submission, an identity verification, a fully staffed survivor-support program, a consent registry, or a mechanism that can force already distributed media to disappear.

Original testimony, child-identifying information, private evidence, and footage are not collected here. There is no text input, no account, no recording and no external dispatch. Every future field activity requires independent human permission.

JAR holds what matters. VIS attends to what is possible. The crossing belongs to those concerned.
