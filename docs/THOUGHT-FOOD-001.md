# THOUGHT-FOOD-001 — JAR;VIS Creative Pantry

**Review-only, 2026-10-29.** Curated public-source ideas from owner-native film, book, music-film, remixed record, synthetic sound-world and festival-fork experiments enter a deterministic *thought-food* compiler. This does **not** import or train on personal audio, copyrighted films/books, survivor accounts, children's identities, or connected private files.

## What is working
`node tools/thought-food.mjs demo packet.json` writes a portable held packet with six-way source ancestry options, default three-source crossing questions, exact public PR-head commit references, independent gate language and a SHA-256 local-integrity receipt. `node tools/thought-food.mjs verify packet.json` recomposes from the local compiled catalog and rejects changes. Use `node --test test/thought-food.test.mjs` for reproducibility, tamper resistance, zero-authority and license-boundary tests. No external requests. Packet text is original short paraphrase and new creative questions, not copied media.

The default composition is: six original native Haunted Toaster films + original held lemonPRESS illustrated machine-book edition + original webZ festival fork. Further selectable stems: webZ's local music-film recipe, lemonPRESS's history-driven synthetic record remix, and CANNON's synthetic Groovetown sounds.

## Why for Voqal
JAR;VIS uses fiction, film grammar, machine folios and music **as lenses for questions**, not answers. The resulting *Second Table* problem is actionable: can a newcomer take a small public-safe method, alter/refuse it, and create a new gathering with separate local governance and no inherited authority? The packet is a conceptual offer to an applicant, independent steward or new circle. Its record of a fictional prototype is never evidence of actual community impact.

## Boundaries
- Source references are pinned as observed GitHub PR heads at authoring; the CLI does **not** re-fetch or cryptographically verify the Git objects. Local digest is not a signature, identity, third-party review or rights certificate.
- The compiler does **not** invoke the original owner engines; their PRs are distinct, mostly unmerged. None of the artifacts or prompts is permission to render, publish, distribute, invite, enroll, fund or submit.
- No private input, real persons, personal case histories, external dispatch, names, addresses, contact lists or web tracking.
- In a real community, participants and independent safeguarding/accessibility reviewers can veto or adapt the method. Sensitive survivor pathways must remain separate from public entertainment.
- A receiving app must inspect the packet independently. Its local checksum alone does not prove where it came from.

**Status: executable local creative *ideation* with synthetic/no-human outcome, not an operating community or submitted fellowship.**
