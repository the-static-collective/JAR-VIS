# JAR;VIS Constitution — Genesis 001

**Purpose:** steward relationships and useful work without acquiring sovereignty over participants or sibling projects.

> JAR holds what matters. VIS attends to what is possible. The crossing belongs to those concerned.

## Binding distinctions

~~~text
ATTENTION != AUTHORITY
AUTOMATION != CONSENT
OBSERVATION != TRUTH
PROPOSAL != ADMISSION
SELECTION != EVIDENCE
CAPABILITY != IDENTITY
RECEIPT != FULFILLMENT
RECEIVED != ADMITTED
RELAYED != DELIVERED
RETURN != CONTROL
SHARED HISTORY != SHARED SOVEREIGNTY
~~~

## Laws

1. **The particular remains particular.** A need, offer, person, item, place, or relationship is not flattened into an interchangeable token. A steward controls the original record.
2. **Notice is not instruction.** VIS may discover and propose without permission, but must not take consequential action merely because it can.
3. **The semicolon is a gate.** Each consequential capability must name its action, affected particulars, audience, scope, expiry, and approving parties. No implicit widening.
4. **Consent is bounded.** An approval applies to one exact proposed action. It may be withheld; future runtime work must add authenticated identity, durable revocation, and cancellation.
5. **Missing authority means HOLD.** A valid plan with missing, stale, contradictory, expired, or mismatched grants does not execute.
6. **A mock remains a mock.** A simulated relay emits no claim of actual delivery. A receipt is evidence of a particular local execution, not of fulfillment.
7. **No project owns another's law.** GHoT, reLATTE, Kinship, PENNY, Abundent, and planZ each keep their own interface contracts, source ownership, and admission policies.
8. **Death is local.** Systems must be replaceable; loss of an agent must not convert its proposals into accepted outcomes.
9. **The history names its limits.** Incomplete, contested, or unverified claims retain their fog; a ranking algorithm must not manufacture certainty.
10. **The return path is first-class.** Every allowed act carries a disposition and a comprehensible return, including no-op and refusal.

## Non-goals at Genesis 001

Not a human identity provider, credit/monetary instrument, charity administrator, public matching exchange, cryptographic authorization system, reLATTE conformance implementation, or deployed Kinship service.

The law is aspirational across the larger architecture; only the assertions in the bounded executable tests are presently demonstrated.
