## Stop signs

- **Child safety and family privacy:** no identifying child data, photographs, addresses, dietary or medical records, family history, need eligibility scoring, unsolicited assistance or unauthorized referrals.
- **Actual nutrition and food handling:** software recipes and AI labels are not clinical nutrition advice or proof of safe food distribution. Qualified reviewers and consent are prerequisites.
- **Identity and autonomy:** a supposed helper report is not recipient confirmation; receipts are not the meal; a person may refuse the table or join independently.
- **Institutional honesty:** Coalition of Care core-team involvement and individual conversations are applicant self-reports; they do not constitute Coalition sponsorship, university approval, food-shelf authorization or Voqal acceptance.
- **Governance:** no automatic planZ task creation, cross-repo write, Jubilee/GrO canon, donor financial claim or public application submission.
