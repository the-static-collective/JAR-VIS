## Narrative justice, correctly bounded

Voqal's grantmaking page https://voqalpartners.org/grantmaking/ discusses narrative justice in the sense of a community's power over its representation. This is not evidence of how any particular fellowship reviewer scores an application.

The Still Here / Second Table candidate asks whether someone with a practical need can choose an invitation, retain meaningful authorship over personal accounts, refuse filming while still participating in useful community work, correct a portrayal, and possibly establish a second independent gathering. That human experiment has **not happened**.
